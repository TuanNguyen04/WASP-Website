Coming soon WASP-B · Plants

How do you see the green world?

WASP-B brings plants into the World Archives of Species Perception: roughly 2,000 plant species, representing about 76% of all plant families evaluated under the IUCN Red List. It follows the spirit of WASP-A, with a more in-depth survey design.

What to expect

Plants deserve a closer look

Plants are often overlooked in conservation debates, a phenomenon sometimes called “plant blindness”. WASP-B will help researchers understand which plants people notice, value and want to protect.

Rate plant species

Observe real photographs of plants from around the world and tell us what you think. There are no right or wrong answers.

A deeper survey

WASP-B builds on WASP-A with a more sophisticated survey design that captures richer insights into how people perceive plants.

Grow your journal

Plants you rate will join your personal field journal, with new badges to unlock along the way.
