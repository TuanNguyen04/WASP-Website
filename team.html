Who is who

The WASP team

Economists, biologists and data scientists at the Centre for Environmental Sciences (CMK), Hasselt University.

RM

Robert Malina

Full Professor in Environmental Economics · Director of CMK

Robert previously worked at Muenster University in Germany as a Lecturer in Economics and at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology as Associate Director of the Laboratory for Aviation and the Environment. Robert brings to the WASP project his expertise in non-market valuation, more specifically on the value of “nature”.

MV

Maarten Vanhove

Assistant Professor in Biology · Research Group Zoology, CMK

Maarten previously worked at the Royal Museum for Central Africa (Belgium), the Hellenic Centre for Marine Research (Greece), Masaryk University (Czech Republic), the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences, and the Finnish Museum of Natural History within the University of Helsinki. He brings to the WASP project not only his experience as a biodiversity scientist, but also his international network in conservation and development cooperation.

TN

Tuan Nguyen

PhD researcher

Tuan completed his Master’s degree in Economics & Ecology, an interdisciplinary programme co-organised by Toulouse School of Economics and Paul Sabatier University (France). He embarked on the WASP project as a PhD student and brings his enthusiasm and multidisciplinary view, combining recent progress in environmental economics, ecology and biodiversity conservation.

IM

Ilias Mokas

Data-driven modelling of human choices

Ilias specialises in the data-driven mathematical modelling of human choices. With expertise in discrete choice analysis and machine learning, he focuses on the estimation and interpretation of such models across different disciplines, although most of his applied work has been in environmental economics. He previously worked at the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre and Copenhagen Business School.
